Risk Mitigation Plan

This Risk Mitigation Plan summarizes cyber risk findings from the sample mission assessment. The draft reflects evidence available through the initial index baseline; subsequent updates incorporate newly ingested findings only.

Executive Summary

Three high-priority findings were identified during the baseline assessment: unrestricted RDP exposure on the perimeter firewall, a weak password policy without MFA, and critical patch backlog on server SRV-01.

Findings

Finding 1: Firewall allows RDP from 0.0.0.0/0 to internal hosts (Risk: High). Finding 2: Weak password policy with no complexity or expiry (Risk: Medium). Finding 3: Server SRV-01 had 15 critical Windows updates pending (Risk: High).

Recommendations

Restrict RDP to management VLAN or VPN only. Enforce NIST-compliant password policy and deploy MFA. Establish a patch management schedule for SRV-01 and peer servers.
